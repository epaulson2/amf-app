## Executive summary
Most of the code is present and deployed. The 404s are not missing deployments: the 14 DADGAD day pages and `/musical-universe/practice` have **no code** (absent code); weeks 3–12 and days 13–78 have code but **no data** (`lib/curriculum-data.ts` holds days 1–12 only); two system links point at the **wrong path**. The bigger gap is not a 404: a third of the live pages are notice-only shells.
