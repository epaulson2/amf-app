Moving apart: the tritone

Look again at Journey 1. Begin with C4 and E4. The lower voice drops to B3, the B just below C. The upper voice rises to F4. They move in opposite directions, and the space between them opens.

HOW TO READ THIS PICTURE

The arrows above the keys show the two voices leaving C4 and E4 in opposite directions. The keys B3 and F4 are tinted with the tritone colour, and the six numbered arcs count every key from B3 to F4. The chip is a separate historic lens, not the colour. These are B3 and F4, not the C4 and F♯4 on the Palette card.

On your keyboard. Play C4 and E4. Move the lower voice down to B3 and the upper voice up to F4 at the same time. Play the new pair and count six semitone steps from B3 to F4. Then notice how you feel about it on your sound, and write one word if you like. There is no required answer.
