# Lesson 1B: static teaching notes

Half and whole steps, the major-scale pattern, open endings and arrivals.

Static lesson notes that feed the later teaching retrofit. The website explains; the learner plays everything on their **own keyboard**. No toggles, reveals, quizzes or sound.

## B01 · Open Ending versus Arrival
- **Status:** ART GENERATED + NATIVE READY (ART) — early answer-neutral page and later pair
- **Placement:** 1B after prediction question is complete
- **Learning outcome:** See two related endings with shared beginning but different final destination.
- **How to read the picture:** Native accurate W1 fixture pair: B01 seven rising upper notes C D E F G A B; B02 adds higher C only. Render early page B01 path alone; later static B01/B02 side-by-side with C final labeled.
- **On your own keyboard:** First learner plays C through B and notices open stopping point without seeing the arrival answer; after reading next static page plays higher C afterward and compares.
- **Truth guard:** No interactive reveal or pre-exposure of higher C in first question. Subjective completeness varies.

## B02 · Where Half Steps Really Are
- **Status:** NATIVE READY (NATIVE)
- **Placement:** 1B half-step introduction
- **Learning outcome:** Find a semitone as the immediate neighboring physical key, including white-white E–F and B–C.
- **How to read the picture:** Static precise E–F and B–C adjacency, C–C♯ as chromatic neighboring keys, and note group geometry. Label 1 semitone in each case.
- **On your own keyboard:** Explain that adjacent keys are always one semitone even if both are white; learner plays C then black key, E then F and B then C.
- **Truth guard:** Do not render black key between E–F or B–C. Do not substitute equal white-key spacing for interval measurement.
- **Files:** `public/soundscape/m03/native/B02-native.svg`, `public/soundscape/m03/native/B02-native.print.svg`

## B03 · Whole Step = Two Semitones
- **Status:** NATIVE READY (NATIVE)
- **Placement:** 1B after B02
- **Learning outcome:** Trace two neighboring chromatic key moves to travel a whole step.
- **How to read the picture:** Static C→C♯→D (two semitone hops), C→D total 2, also optional E→F♯ and truthful black key. Native endpoint and intermediate key labels.
- **On your own keyboard:** Guide the learner to play C, the immediately neighboring black key, then D, and contrast with C straight to D.
- **Truth guard:** Never imply skipping one white key universally equals whole step.

## B05 · Major Scale: Unequal Step Garland
- **Status:** ART GENERATED + NATIVE READY (ART)
- **Placement:** 1B after C-major formula introduction
- **Learning outcome:** See one coherent scale with intentional unequal step sizes, not arbitrary seven equal segments.
- **How to read the picture:** Draw accurate static C D E F G A B C with measured semitone hop pattern W W H W W W H (2,2,1,2,2,2,1); native keyboard and classifying labels. Generated petal distances artistic only; a separate native ruler proves the count.
- **On your own keyboard:** Walk the major scale with keyboard, noticing E–F and B–C half steps; try from a different pitch only as optional curiosity with physical guidance.
- **Truth guard:** Do not assign petal width quantitative meaning unless native measured overlay specifically enforces it.
