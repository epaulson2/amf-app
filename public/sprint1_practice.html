Sprint 1 Anchor Song: Ain't No Sunshine — Bill Withers

Progression: Am – Em – G – Am · Key: A minor · Tempo: 76 BPM

This song appears in every section of every week. You won't just learn it — you'll learn through it.

The Non-Negotiable: Hear or sing every note before you play it. Every exercise.

Three Definitions of Done: Mastery (9/10, no hesitation, at tempo) · Progressive (better than last time) · Performance Ready (from memory, in front of someone, without stopping)

Musical OS · Plogger Ch. 1–2

The Three Stages of Learning

Stage 1
Discovery
First contact. No pressure to be right. Keep going — don't stop.
Stage 2
Consolidation
Sometimes know it. Slow down. Repetition is the tool here.
Stage 3
Mastery
Always know. Automatic. 9/10 at full tempo, no hesitation.

The rule: Always stay at your actual stage. Never perform in Stage 1. Never coast in Stage 3.

Melody Chamber — Zone System

The Four Zones in A Minor

Z1 — Sweet
A · C · E
Stable. Home. These notes always feel right.
Z2 — Bitter
B · G
Reaching. Mild tension. Pulls back to Z1.
Z3 — Tense
D · F
Strong pull. Demands resolution to Z1.
Z4 — Wild
G# · Bb
Outside the key. Use intentionally.

Tiny Tension Arc: Z1 → Z1 → Z2 → Z1. Stability · Stability · Reach · Home. The minimum complete musical statement.

Harmony Chamber — Di-Chord Quality

Major vs. Minor — The Acoustic Truth

[3] Minor 3rd
A + C = Am quality
Non-harmonic. Closed. Acidic.
[4] Major 3rd
A + C# = A major
Harmonic. Open. Alkaline.
[7] Perfect 5th
A + E = pure open
Perfectly stable. The "open" interval.
Rhythm Chamber — Two Lenses

Longy Syllables + Rhythm Code Grid

Longy — "What is it?"

Name the rhythm by how it sounds.

ta = quarter note  ·  ta-te = two eighths
ta---te = dotted quarter + eighth
Rhythm Code — "Where does it sit?"

8 positions per 4/4 bar: 1=beat1, 2="and"1, 3=beat2, 4="and"2, etc.

Stop = last note before a rest.  Anticipation = note starting on an "and."

Plogger Foundation

Keyboard Visualization Drill 5 min S1

Close your eyes. Picture the keyboard. Answer aloud — no hesitation:

  1. C is to the ___ of the two black keys.
  2. D is ___ the two black keys.
  3. F is to the ___ of the three black keys.
  4. Name all natural notes: C · D · E · ___ · G · A · ___
  5. Solfège for A? For C? For G?

Target: No hesitation. Any pause = Stage 2 work next session.

Longy + Rhythm Code — Two Lenses, One Pattern 5 min S1

Metronome 70 BPM. Read each pattern with both tools at once — Longy names it, Rhythm Code places it on the 8-position grid.

2
Section 2
Integrated Work
18 min
Reconnection — Open Chords + Alternating Bass 8 min S1

Right hand THUMB ONLY for first 4 minutes. Thumb independence must be automatic before anything else is added.

  • G chord → Alternate: string 6 (beat 1) · string 4 (beat 3). 60 BPM, 4 bars.
  • C chord → Alternate: string 5 (beat 1) · string 4 (beat 3). 4 bars.
  • Am chord → Alternate: string 5 (beat 1) · string 3 (beat 3). 4 bars.

Add one finger: After thumb on beat 1, index plucks string 1 on the "and" of beat 1. Pattern: Thumb-INDEX · Thumb · Thumb-INDEX · Thumb. 4 bars on Am. Hear before you play: sing the root note before each chord change.

Major/Minor Ear Training — Di-Chord Foundation 5 min S2

Play Am. Hold — feel the closed, acidic quality. Play A major (A-C#-E). Hold — feel the open quality. Alternate 8 times, eyes closed. Before each: anticipate the sound, then play and check.

Repeat with Em vs. E major. Feel [3] (minor third) vs. [4] (major third) as physical sensations.

Am Progression — Clean Transitions 5 min S2

Play Am–Em–G–Am. Two down-strums per chord, 70 BPM. No gap between chords. The next chord must be forming before the current one releases. Diagnose errors: late on Em = Reaction (slow down). Knew you'd miss G while still on Em = Anticipation (stay present).

Section 4
Jamming / Free Play
10 min
Rhythm Code Groove Drill — Ain't No Sunshine 5 min S1

Hold an Am chord. Apply both lenses to the anchor song's groove:

Step 1: Clap "Ain't no sunshine when she's gone." Speak Longy syllables aloud while clapping.

Step 2: Find where "gone" lands — that's the Stop. Last note before the rest. Feel the phrase settle there.

Step 3: Try shifting "when she's" to start one eighth note early (anticipation). Does it feel more pushed, more alive?

The point: hearing how grid position changes the feel of the same notes. Rhythm Code in action.

Pentatonic Improv + Free Play 5 min S1

3 min: A minor pentatonic over Am–Em–G–Am. Sing before you play. Notice Zone 1 (home: A, C, E) vs. reaching notes (D, G).

2 min: Free play. No rules. Just you and the guitar.

Section 5
The Synthesizer
8 min
All Systems — Zone 1 Arpeggio + Rhythm Grid 8 min S1

This is where all four chambers meet simultaneously in one simple exercise.

The Exercise: Play the three Zone 1 notes of Am (A · C · E) as a slow arpeggio — one note per beat — while simultaneously engaging all four systems:

Musical OS: Name the di-chord aloud: A=[0] (unison), C=[3] (minor/closed), E=[7] (open).
Melody: All Zone 1. Feel pure stability — no tension, pure home.
Harmony: You're arpeggating Am — harmonic content expressed melodically.
Rhythm: Pass 1 = ta·ta·ta. Pass 2 = ta-te·ta-te·ta. Pass 3 = dotted feel.

The insight: Same three notes · Same chord · Same zones — completely different character from rhythm alone. Four chambers, one gesture.

Guitar session complete — 60 min. Before closing: speak one Longy pattern, sing one Zone 1 note, name the di-chord quality of the minor third you played today.

Section 1
Plogger Foundation
10 min
Keyboard Visualization + Touch 4 min S1

You have the actual keyboard — use it physically. Trace answers with your finger as you answer aloud:

  1. Touch C. Now touch every C across the keyboard. How many are there?
  2. Touch F on three different octaves. Confirm: F is always left of the three-black group.
  3. Starting from middle C, name every white key going up: doh re mi fa sol la si doh.
  4. Close eyes. Find middle C by feel alone — landmark method, no peeking.
Longy + Rhythm Code at the Keyboard 6 min S1

Same combined drill as guitar — but the piano lets you hear it immediately. 70 BPM. Speak syllables while playing single notes on middle C:

Eighthsta-te · ta-te · ta-te · ta-teAll 8 positionsTwice as active. Every "and" filled.
Mixta · ta-te · ta · ta-te1 · 3,4 · 5 · 7,8Quarter + pair alternating.

Apply to Ain't No Sunshine: Clap the vocal rhythm at the keyboard lid. Speak Longy. Then play it on a single note (C). Find the Stop after "gone" — mark its grid position.

2
Section 2
Integrated Work
18 min
Chord Transitions — Inversions + Voice Economy 8 min S2

Chords: Am · Dm · E7 · Am. Make transitions conscious and ear-connected.

Step 1 (2 min): Play each chord and speak quality: "Am — minor, closed" · "Dm — minor, closed" · "E7 — dominant seventh, tense."

Step 2 (3 min): LH plays root notes (A·D·E·A), quarter notes, 65 BPM. RH plays chords with inversions — no RH jump larger than a 3rd. Am root (A-C-E) → Dm 1st inv (F-A-D, A stays) → E7 shell (E-G#-D) → Am.

Step 3 (3 min): Hear before you play. Sing the root note of the incoming chord before each change. Then play it.

Major/Minor Ear Training at Keyboard 6 min S2

Play A (single note). Then C above it — non-harmonic, closed, acidic. Now C# above A — harmonic, open. Alternate A+C → A+C# → A+C → A+C#, 8 times. Eyes closed. Predict the quality before each.

Extend to full chords: Am vs. A major, 8 alternations. Not "Am = sad" — "Am = non-harmonic third."

Two-Hand Independence — LH Pulse + RH Chords 4 min S2

LH plays A in steady quarter notes, 65 BPM. RH plays Am chord on beats 1 and 3. LH pulse must not break when RH changes rhythm. Then RH on every beat. Then RH on 2 and 4 only. The LH is the metronome — RH must not pull it off the pulse.

Section 5
The Synthesizer
8 min
All Systems — Zone 1 Arpeggio + Two Hands 8 min S1

Same concept as guitar — but the piano lets both hands work simultaneously, making each system immediately audible.

The Exercise: LH holds Am chord (root position, sustained). RH arpeggates Zone 1 notes (A · C · E) — one note per beat — engaging all four chambers:

Musical OS: Name each di-chord aloud as RH plays. A=[0] (unison), C=[3] (minor/closed), E=[7] (open).
Melody: All Zone 1. Feel pure stability — you are playing the notes of least tension against the chord.
Harmony: RH spells the Am chord melodically while LH holds it harmonically. Same content, two simultaneous voices.
Rhythm: Pass 1 = ta·ta·ta. Pass 2 = ta-te·ta-te·ta (eighths on A+C, quarter on E). Pass 3 = try a dotted feel.

The insight: LH gives you the harmonic anchor while RH explores the rhythmic surface. Same three notes, completely different character — that's the Synthesizer at work.

Piano session complete — 60 min. Before closing the lid: name the di-chord quality of the C you played against A today. Play it once more. Feel open or closed?

Weeks 2–4 — Concept Progression

Each week follows the same 5-section structure. The Plogger exercises and chamber concepts advance. The anchor song deepens.

Week 2

Keyboard Mastery · Three Causes · Zone 1 Fluency

Plogger (Section 1)

Ch.2 — Three Causes: Diagnose your own errors by cause before fixing them. Write one example of each cause from Week 1.

Ch.3 Complete: All 12 note names by landmark. Eyes-closed drills. Target: name any note in <2 seconds.

Longy + Rhythm Code: All Week 1 patterns plus dotted quarter+eighth (ta---te). Map the full Ain't No Sunshine verse rhythm to the grid.

Melody (Section 2)

Zone 1 fluency: Over Am drone, play only A/C/E. Sing before each note. Find them in 2+ positions on both instruments. Target: immediate location.

Harmony (Sections 2–3)

Am progression at 90 BPM: No gaps. Guitar: Travis picking intro (thumb alternating + first treble note). Piano: Shell voicing Am with simple LH bass (A quarter notes).

Synthesizer (Section 5)

Rhythm variation: Add ta-te on A-C, then quarter on E. Can you hear when [3] feels "brighter" at a faster rhythmic position vs. sitting on a slow beat?

Week 3

Longy Rhythms Deep · Zones 1–3 · Chord Changes at Tempo

Plogger (Section 1)

Ch.4 Longy deep: All Week 1 patterns plus dotted quarter+eighth, eighth+dotted quarter (ta te---), syncopated off-beat eighths. Apply all to Ain't No Sunshine.

Ch.3 consolidation: Eyes-closed — all 12 chromatic notes. No hesitation on C#/Db, F#/Gb.

Melody — Zones 1–3

Zone 2 (B, G over Am): Reaching but not tense. Zone 3 (D over Am): Strong pull to resolve. Tiny Tension Arc: A–A–B–A (Z1–Z1–Z2–Z1). Sing it. Then play it.

Harmony — Full Song Form

"I know" section: Pure Am vamp — 26 repetitions. Play as a groove: Am repeating with rhythmic LH pattern (piano) / Travis picking (guitar). Groove through repetition.

Rhythm Code (Section 4)

Strong vs. Medium positions: Positions 1,3,5,7 = strong beats. 2,4,6,8 = "and" positions. Which positions does the "I know" vocal pattern use? Mark them on a blank grid.

Synthesizer (Section 5) — Week 3

Build the Tiny Tension Arc as a multi-chamber event: Zone 1 notes + Am harmonic context + a specific Longy rhythm pattern (ta-te·ta·ta-te·ta). Four chambers, one phrase. Sing it first, then play it.

Week 4

Integration Week · Full Song · Sprint Exit

Week 4 Mission: Put It All Together

No new concepts this week — deepen what you have. By end of Week 4 you should be able to: play through Ain't No Sunshine completely on both instruments, apply Longy syllables to its main rhythmic patterns, identify the Am minor quality by ear instantly, improvise a simple phrase using Zone 1–2 notes over the Am vamp, and complete the Synthesizer Section 5 with all four chambers simultaneously.

🎸 Guitar Week 4 Focus

  • Travis picking clean at 80 BPM over 4-chord loop
  • Full song form with alternating bass — no stopping
  • Record yourself once. Listen back.
  • Section 5: Zone 1 arpeggio over full Am–Em–G–Am with rhythm variations
Synthesizer Challenge: Play Zone 1 arpeggio (A·C·E) over the full progression. Notice: C is [3] over Am but becomes [8] over Em. Hear the di-chord shift as the bass changes.

🎹 Piano Week 4 Focus

  • Conjunct Am heptachord: ascending + descending without looking
  • Full verse progression LH+RH at 80 BPM, inversions throughout
  • Chord tone improv RH over LH chords
  • "I know" section as groove for 2 full minutes
Synthesizer Challenge: LH holds each chord of the progression while RH plays Zone 1 arpeggio. Notice how the di-chord relationships change with the bass.

Week 4 Jam Prompt — Full Integration

Improvise over Am–Em–G–Am for 5 minutes with no planning. When you land on a note, name its zone. When you play a chord, name one di-chord interval in it. When you feel a groove, identify the grid position of the first note. You are not stopping to analyze — you are practicing awareness in motion. This is what The Synthesizer becomes at Stage 3.

Week 4 Synthesizer — Full Circuit

Guitar: Travis picking and Zone 1 melodic improvisation simultaneously — picking hand delivers the Rhythm Code groove while fretting hand thinks in zones.  |  Piano: LH bass in Rhythm Code positions + RH chord tone melody in zones. Name one di-chord interval each time you change bass notes.
