Ajourné into Music

How learning works here

  1. Read a short, plain explanation.
  2. See the picture and learn how to read it.
  3. Play it on your own keyboard. The website never plays sound for you.
  4. Notice what you hear and what changed.
  5. Create something small of your own.

Pages are paged with ordinary Next and Previous links. There is nothing to click to reveal an answer.
